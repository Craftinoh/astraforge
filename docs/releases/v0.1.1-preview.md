# AstraForge v0.1.1-preview: stability, skin-bake sources, measurement tools

**Pre-release.** This is a diagnostic preview for contributors and testers. It is not a finished or validated build.

## What changed since v0.1.0-preview

- **Skin-bake sources (fxceless `b3d49db`, `082ebaf`, `2983a71`).** Before every character skin or decoration bake, each source texture the bake reads is checked. A stale source is uploaded on the bake's own list, whatever the texture room. This covers views showing another texture, textures without a host copy, and host copies with old bytes. In each automated run the check brought about 180 stale sources up to date, with no failures. `VICTORY_BAKE_SOURCES=0` restores the old behaviour.
- **Shader translation (Vice-Tay #315).** Scalar operand codes above 127 no longer index past the spill table, and `s_min`/`s_max` now set SCC as the RDNA 2 ISA specifies.
- **Card-copy sends (Vice-Tay #286).** Sends are laid out in one pass, and ranges that are already ordered skip the sort. No measurable FPS change on this PC.
- **Measurement tools:**
  - The launcher prefers the official PresentMon 2.x over the FrameView SDK copy.
  - The launcher stops its ETW session at exit; force-stopped runs had left sessions running.
  - Shared binaries no longer embed the builder's user path.

Credits and exact provenance are in `docs/source-credits.md` and `third_party/nvidia-3070-VI/LOCAL-CHANGES.md`.

## Observed on one RTX 3070 8 GB / Ryzen 7 7700X / 32 GiB PC (720p, Stability profile)

- **Stability:** no crash in 10 automated runs, 6 of them with this release's source. Each reached the world about 55 s after launch and played the first story scene for 2–3 minutes. The warmed shader cache and pipeline library were used.
- **Internal frame reports** (the runtime's own 5-second lines, not PresentMon): 10–12 fps on average. Earlier, one PresentMon capture measured 9.8 fps average, 11.6 median and 2.4 for the 1% low.
- **Frame budget:**
  - about 55–70 ms of CPU-side preparation (list translation and copies);
  - 30–36 ms copying GPU results back;
  - 7–13 ms on the GPU.
  - The limit is the CPU side, not the graphics card.
- **Texture room:** more room is a large regression on 8 GB cards. At 90 % it measured 5.2 fps, and at 94 % 2.1 fps with 215 ms of GPU time per frame. Keep the **Stability** profile (80 %) on 8 GB.

## Known limits

- **Black character skin is intermittent.** With identical settings, some runs show correct skin and others black skin, while eyes, lips, hair and clothes are correct. The bake-sources port fixes one cause, but one of three diagnostic runs still showed black skin.
- **Flickering**, and materials or colours missing on some objects.
- **Low frame rate**, CPU-bound as described above. No FPS target is claimed.
- **PresentMon** often loses ETW events in this workload (0.3–0.9 M per 90 s capture) and then writes no CSV.
- The first launch after a new build rebuilds shaders and pipelines, and may hit the title's watchdog during story load. Launch again, or prewarm with `tools/Prewarm-AstraForge.ps1`.
- Only NVIDIA Ampere was tested. AMD and other GPUs are not supported.

## How to help

Run `tools/Start-AstraForge3070.ps1` with your own legally owned game files at 1280x720 and the Stability profile. Then follow `docs/benchmark-procedure.md`. Report your GPU, driver, profile and what you saw, with logs. Remove private paths and titles, and use anonymous scene labels such as `scene-01`. No game files are distributed here or accepted in reports.
